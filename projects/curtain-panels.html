Curtain Panel Suite

From a design rule to annotated facade drawings.

  • C#
  • Revit API
  • adaptive families
Overview

Large glass facades consist of hundreds of panels, often in many slightly different shapes. Each panel type requires its own drawing, a schedule and dimensions. These four tools create the panels from a design rule and then produce the schedules, drawings and annotations automatically.

The problem

Documenting a complex facade panel by panel, with a schedule, a view and dimensions for every type, is slow and prone to error.

The solution

A chain of tools that takes a curtain wall facade (a facade of glass and frame panels) from a design rule to fully annotated drawings.

Panel Generator creates and edits curtain panels across the facade. Panel Scheduler builds and exports panel schedules. Panel Drawings creates a separate view for every panel type. Panel Tagger tags and dimensions the whole set automatically.

Features

  • Panel Generator: creates and edits curtain panels across the whole facade
  • Panel Scheduler: builds and exports panel schedules
  • Panel Drawings: creates a separate view for every panel type
  • Panel Tagger: labels and dimensions the whole set automatically

Glossary

Revit
Autodesk's main software for designing buildings as BIM models. It is widely used by architects and engineers.
Schedule
A table in Revit that lists elements and their data, for example every door with its size.
Curtain wall
A building facade made of glass and frame panels hung on the structure, as on most office towers.
